# GOVENICS GRC — Statutory & Regulatory Research References

This document maintains authoritative research references, statutory citations, acts, rules, and regulatory portals used in developing the 40 GOVENICS LinkedIn Articles.

---

## 1. Central Government Authorities & Portals

### Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA)
- **Primary Legislation**: Companies Act, 2013 (Act No. 18 of 2013)
- **Key Sections Cited**:
  - Section 134(5)(f): Directors' Responsibility Statement regarding statutory compliance systems.
  - Section 135: Corporate Social Responsibility rules, Schedule VII activities, and impact assessments.
  - Section 149 & 177: Independent directors and Audit Committee oversight.
  - Section 178: Nomination and Remuneration Committee governance.
  - Section 188: Related Party Transactions approvals and disclosures.
- **Official URL**: [https://www.mca.gov.in/](https://www.mca.gov.in/)
- **Statutory Repository**: [India Code MCA](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2114)

### Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)
- **Primary Regulations**:
  - SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (LODR).
  - Regulation 23: Related Party Transactions policy and omnibus approvals.
  - Regulation 30: Material event disclosure timelines and quantitative thresholds.
  - Regulation 34(2)(f) & Circular dated July 12, 2023: Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR Core) and assurance roadmap.
- **Official URL**: [https://www.sebi.gov.in/](https://www.sebi.gov.in/)

### Ministry of Labour and Employment (MoLE)
- **Central Legislation**:
  - Code on Wages, 2019 (Act No. 29 of 2019)
  - Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (Act No. 37 of 2020)
  - Code on Social Security, 2020 (Act No. 36 of 2020)
  - Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (Act No. 35 of 2020)
  - Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970 (Act No. 37 of 1970)
  - Factories Act, 1948 (Act No. 63 of 1948)
  - Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952
  - Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948
- **Official URL**: [https://labour.gov.in/](https://labour.gov.in/) | [Chief Labour Commissioner](https://clc.gov.in/)

### Ministry of Women and Child Development
- **Legislation**: Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (POSH Act).
- **Mandates**: Internal Committee (IC) constitution, conciliation vs inquiry procedures, annual district officer reporting, Section 134 board report disclosures.
- **Official URL**: [https://wcd.nic.in/](https://wcd.nic.in/)

### Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) & Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)
- **Legislation & Rules**:
  - Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 (Act No. 29 of 1986)
  - Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974
  - Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981
  - Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules, 2016
  - Plastic Waste Management Rules, 2016 (and EPR amendments)
  - E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022
  - Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022
  - Manufacture, Storage and Import of Hazardous Chemical Rules, 1989 (MSIHC)
  - Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) Notification, 2006
- **Official Portals**:
  - PARIVESH Single Window Environmental Clearances: [https://parivesh.nic.in/](https://parivesh.nic.in/)
  - CPCB Central Portal: [https://cpcb.nic.in/](https://cpcb.nic.in/)
  - CPCB EPR Portal: [https://eprplastic.cpcb.gov.in/](https://eprplastic.cpcb.gov.in/)

### Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organization (PESO) & DPIIT
- **Legislation**:
  - Petroleum Act, 1934 and Petroleum Rules, 2002
  - Explosives Act, 1884 and Explosives Rules, 2008
  - Static and Mobile Pressure Vessels (Unfired) Rules, 2016
- **Official URL**: [https://peso.gov.in/](https://peso.gov.in/) | [DPIIT](https://dpiit.gov.in/)

### Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)
- **Legislation**: Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (Act No. 34 of 2006)
- **Key Regulations**: Food Safety and Standards (Licensing and Registration of Food Businesses) Regulations, 2011; Packaging and Labeling Regulations; Food Safety and Standards (Recall Procedure) Regulations, 2017.
- **Official Portals**: [https://www.fssai.gov.in/](https://www.fssai.gov.in/) | [FoSCoS Licensing](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/)

### Department of Consumer Affairs — Legal Metrology
- **Legislation**: Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (Act No. 1 of 2010)
- **Key Rules**: Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (LMPC).
- **Mandates**: Mandatory declarations, net quantity norms, manufacturer/packer details, e-commerce disclosures, Director Nomination under Section 49.
- **Official URL**: [https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/)

### Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) & GST Council
- **Legislation**:
  - Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (CGST Act)
  - Integrated Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (IGST Act)
  - Customs Act, 1962 (Act No. 52 of 1962)
- **Key Compliance Mechanisms**: Section 16(2)(aa) input tax credit conditions, GSTR-2B dynamic reconciliation, Rule 48(4) mandatory e-invoicing thresholds, Circular 33/2016 Authorized Economic Operator (AEO T1/T2/T3) framework.
- **Official Portals**: [https://www.cbic.gov.in/](https://www.cbic.gov.in/) | [GST Portal](https://www.gst.gov.in/) | [ICEGATE](https://icegate.gov.in/)

### Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT)
- **Legislation**: Income-tax Act, 1961
- **Key Provisions**:
  - Section 43B(h): Mandatory disallowance of delayed payments to Micro and Small Enterprises beyond Section 15 limits of MSMED Act.
  - Section 92 to 92F: Transfer Pricing, Arm's Length Principle, Advance Pricing Agreements (APA).
  - Section 115BAA: Concessional corporate tax regime (22%) and elimination of MAT under Section 115JB.
- **Official Portal**: [https://incometaxindia.gov.in/](https://incometaxindia.gov.in/)

### Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) & Ministry of Commerce
- **Policy**: Foreign Trade Policy 2023 (FTP 2023).
- **Schemes**: Advance Authorization, Export Promotion Capital Goods (EPCG) Scheme, Duty Remission (RoDTEP), Special Economic Zones (SEZ Act 2005).
- **Official Portal**: [https://www.dgft.gov.in/](https://www.dgft.gov.in/) | [SEZ Online](https://www.sezonline-ndml.co.in/)

### Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
- **Statutory Directions**:
  - Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (FEMA) and Current/Capital Account Regulations.
  - Master Direction – Information Technology Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Practices (Nov 2023).
  - Scale Based Regulation (SBR) for Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs).
  - Escrow account guidelines for cross-border transactions and overseas direct investments.
- **Official Portal**: [https://www.rbi.org.in/](https://www.rbi.org.in/)

### Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) & MeitY
- **Statutory Directions**:
  - Section 70B of the Information Technology Act, 2000.
  - CERT-In Directions No. 20(3)/2022-CERT-In dated 28.04.2022: Mandatory 6-hour cybersecurity incident reporting, 180-day system log preservation, NTP synchronization.
  - Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, Act No. 22 of 2023): Data Fiduciary obligations, Data Protection Officer appointments, Data Protection Board enforcement.
- **Official Portals**: [https://www.cert-in.org.in/](https://www.cert-in.org.in/) | [MeitY](https://www.meity.gov.in/)

## 2. International Standards & Frameworks Cited

- **COSO Enterprise Risk Management Framework**: Integrating with Strategy and Performance
- **ISO 22301:2019**: Security and resilience — Business continuity management systems
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022**: Information security, cybersecurity and privacy protection
- **AICPA SOC 2 Type II**: Trust Services Criteria for Security, Availability, and Confidentiality
- **European Union Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (EU CSDDD)**: Value chain human rights and environmental due diligence.
- **GHG Protocol**: Corporate Standard (Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 supplier footprint accounting).
